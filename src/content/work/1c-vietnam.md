## The brief
1C Vietnam provides business management software and ERP solutions for Vietnamese enterprises. Building on the brand positioning developed with Richard Moore Associates, which defined 1C Vietnam as a comprehensive business management ecosystem with 1C:ERP as its hero product, our task was to turn that positioning into a clear communication strategy: a big idea, key messages, a communication framework and the activities to bring it to life.

## Market research
Medium and upper-medium enterprises are the main drivers of digital transformation in Vietnam, but most lack the resources and standardized processes to make it work. I found a clear gap between what software promises and what businesses actually get: slow adoption, low ROI, employee resistance, and ERP systems running at only 60–80% efficiency because they were forced to follow messy manual workflows.

## Competitor analysis
Reviewing MISA, Base, BRAVO, Odoo and SAP, I noticed the whole market speaks the same language: “comprehensive”, “all-in-one”, “holistic”. Everyone talks about the product, very few talk about what the customer actually needs. That sameness was 1C’s opportunity.

## Audience & insights
B2B buying involves several people, so I mapped four stakeholder groups, each with its own truth, tension and need: decision makers (CEO, CFO) who want control and clarity, technical evaluators (CTO) who want flexible and stable systems, operational leaders who want smoother daily workflows, and synergy partners who want a platform they can build on. I also mapped their media habits, from in-depth reports and case studies to Zalo and Facebook groups. Despite different priorities, they all want one thing: real business impact from digital transformation.

## Communication task
Businesses don’t transform for the sake of transformation. Digital transformation fails not because of technology, but because solutions don’t fit real business needs. So instead of talking about features, we shifted the conversation to business impact, moving 1C from “a digital transformation solution” to “a sustainable business growth enabler.”

## Big idea & messaging
The big idea became “Growth with management-mindset solutions” (Tăng trưởng cùng giải pháp từ tư duy quản lý). From there, I developed a two-phase message structure: “Standard management – Sustainable growth” (Quản lý chuẩn – Tăng trưởng vững) for the master brand, and “Standardized production management – Unlock global integration” for 1C:ERP, targeting Vietnamese manufacturers ready to compete globally.

## Framework & activities
I built a four-stage funnel (Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Adoption) with content pillars and tactics for both customers and partners. Key activities included a brand film showing a 1C expert working alongside businesses across industries, a technical webinar series “Scale Smart with 1C Vietnam”, factory roadshows and lunch-and-learn sessions where decision-makers see 1C:ERP in real operations, a tiered certification program for users and partners, and an always-on B2B social community to educate, engage and generate leads.
