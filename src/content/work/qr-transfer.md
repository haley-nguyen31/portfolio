Scan QR to transfer is one of Zalopay’s core habits, and my goal was to grow and maintain the number of users who adopt it. Through continuous segment analysis and optimization, I drove an average monthly growth in QR transfer users, exceeding the 2025 annual KPI. A highlight was the campaign with Miss Nguyễn Hoàng Phương Linh, encouraging users to scan any QR code with Zalopay. Beyond campaigns, I ran user research and in-depth interviews with QR transfer users to understand their real habits and frustrations, then worked closely with the Product Owner and UX/UI team to turn those findings into product improvements.
