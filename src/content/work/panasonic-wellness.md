## The brief
Panasonic Wellness Community (“Sống khỏe cùng Panasonic”) is the brand’s official Facebook community for home appliance users in Vietnam, already one of the top 3 home appliance communities in the country. Our task was to review its performance and build a full-year development plan for 2025, focused on growing membership and turning a product-led group into a true wellness community.

## Performance review
By the end of 2024, the community had 50,339 members, 5M in awareness and a 10.14% conversion rate. Awareness and conversion beat their KPIs (119% and 105%), but member growth reached only 78% of target. Digging into the content, I found the group was active but relied heavily on seeders and KOCs, with little organic content from real users. Posts explained product features well but rarely touched on lifestyle, and nothing clearly set “wellness” apart from any other appliance group.

## Community research
I analyzed more than 100 wellness groups and leading appliance communities (Samsung, LG, and lifestyle groups with up to 300K members). The groups with the strongest engagement weren’t about products at all: they were places to share daily life, swap practical tips and connect with like-minded people. The lesson was clear: products only create engagement when they’re placed in the context of users’ lives.

## Insight
Wellness is shifting from occasional retreats to an everyday lifestyle, with searches for “wellness travel” declining while “wellness at home” and “home spa” rise. People know wellness matters, but busy lives push them toward quick fixes instead of lasting habits. Wellness isn’t a temporary fix but a daily journey, and the home is where it happens.

## Strategic direction
From that insight came the 2025 platform “The Wellness Journey – Nurturing Habits, Embracing Balance” (Hành trình sống khỏe – Vun đắp thói quen, cân bằng cuộc sống), positioning Panasonic as a daily companion rather than just a product provider. I structured the year into three phases, Join → Engage → Co-create, each with its own content pillar and key activities, with product categories allocated across the year.

## Key activities
The plan included a 48-episode branded series across four wellness solutions (healthy kitchen, home hygiene, clean air, clean water) brought to life by family and lifestyle KOCs; quarterly UGC challenges such as the Fridge Restock Challenge and Homemade Lunchbox Challenge; and minigames to bring in new members.

## Community operations
I designed the always-on content mix (around 109 posts and 1,000+ comments per month), a role structure for admins, seeders and KOC tiers, and a set of mascot “mentors”, one per product category, to make technical product information friendlier. An external recruitment plan through partner pages and lifestyle groups supported the target of growing from 50K to 75K members by early 2026.
