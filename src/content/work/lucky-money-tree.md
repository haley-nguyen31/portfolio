---
For Lunar New Year 2026, we created the “Lucky Money Tree”: every QR transfer unlocked a red envelope, with the Super Cash envelope worth up to 10 million VND, released only on New Year’s Eve to build anticipation.

I owned the planning and paid media for the campaign, from best-practice research (MoMo’s “Lắc Xì”, Alipay’s QR red envelopes) to a two-phase roll-out. The teasing phase featured a social stunt, “Hunt Your Lucky Horse”, where the brand’s horse mascot ran around VNG campus carrying a giant QR envelope, plus a social series about how Vietnamese people give lì xì today. The launch phase combined daily social content, 380+ seeding posts across Facebook groups, TikTok and Threads, and in-app notifications tailored to four user segments.
