A digital-first campaign designed to position La Roche-Posay as the ultimate skincare companion for Gen Z. By  reimagining the brand -  La Roche-Posay as La Roche - Pồ-Say, it will be your-skincare BFF, who:

- Always beside every breakout, every glow-up

By leveraging AI-powered diagnostics, gamification, and social media engagement, we transformed skincare into an interactive, habit-forming experience. The campaign revolved around "Pồ" – a digital skin buddy, offering personalized advice and encouraging daily skincare routines.
