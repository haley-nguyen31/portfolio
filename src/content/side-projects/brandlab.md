101% Brandlab is an educational academy specializing in branding and marketing. Unlike traditional academies, our courses are led by experienced practitioners who are actively working in branding, marketing, and communications (ex-Ogilvy, Anymind Group, Canifa,..). We focus on practical, real-world knowledge, bridging the gap between theory and execution with hands-on case studies and actionable insights. Our mission is to help learners grow 1% better every day with applicable branding strategies and expertise.
