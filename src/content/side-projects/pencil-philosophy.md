Led partnership and communication initiatives with 20+ organizations, enterprises, including a collaboration with the U.S. Embassy in Hanoi.

Designed and executed the communication campaign for Summer Program 2021: reaching over 170,000 young people and generating 1,000+ student registrations nationwide.

## Story context
<!-- Verbatim from About text (portfolio p.2), second paragraph -->
I was project manager for a non-profit bringing liberal arts education to Vietnamese students, founded by Stanford's alumni. In a system where learning often means chasing grades and achievements, many Viet students rarely get the chance to ask “why,” to question, debate, and think independently. In my role, I reached out to sponsors and partners to fund our programs, built collaborations with 20+ organizations including the U.S. Embassy in Hanoi, and led the campaign that brought our Summer Program young students nationwide.
