TALYVI is a digital ecosystem dedicated to enhancing mental well-being for young people while also expanding career opportunities for psychology students and driving the integration of psychology with digital technology. Our core product is a mobile platform that offers personalized mental health tracking, guided self-improvement plans, and expert consultations, helping users take proactive steps toward better mental well-being.
